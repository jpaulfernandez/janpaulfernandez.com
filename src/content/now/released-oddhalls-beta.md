We've released a photo editing app called Oddhalls in beta. It's a local-first desktop editor built for the whole post-shoot workflow: fast culling, raw development with GPU controls and local AI masks, and designing zines, social carousels, and reels. No accounts, no central servers, and your photos stay in your folders.

Check out the app at [oddhalls.janpaulfernandez.com](https://oddhalls.janpaulfernandez.com) or read the write-up under [projects](/projects/oddhalls/).
